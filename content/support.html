Setup & support

Get ready for the next call.

Start with the selected microphone and headphone routes. Keep the catcher involved in the sound check.

Basic setup

  1. Keep the iPhone with the coach. Open the setup guide in PlayCall Settings.
  2. Allow microphone access and choose the intended microphone.
  3. Connect and select the catcher’s Bluetooth headphones, then save the intended pair.
  4. Try the optional audio check. Ask the catcher to confirm sound is clear at a comfortable volume.

If you cannot talk

Microphone access is off
Review iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → PlayCall. If the app is not listed, start microphone setup in the guide.
A device is saved but not active
Reconnect it, then review the actual route in PlayCall. Confirm a replacement pair explicitly. A saved name is not evidence of an active connection.
The catcher cannot hear a test
Check pairing, the selected output, volume and fit. Ask the catcher to listen during another deliberate hold. Do not assume route readiness proves acoustic delivery.
A call was interrupted
Release the button, review the route and readiness state, then make a new press when ready. PlayCall does not automatically resume transmission.

Local reset options

Forget saved audio setup removes the current setup file and keeps preferences and older migration backups.

Clear Local Data asks for confirmation, stops audio, cancels pending setup, and removes saved setup, its migration backup, game and guide preferences, and known temporary files from the retired diagnostic recorder. It does not remove Apple records, iOS permissions, shared reports, or existing in-memory diagnostics. Verify devices again afterward.

Report an issue

A useful report starts small.

Include your app/build and iOS version, what happened, and steps to reproduce. Avoid personal device names, route identifiers, serial numbers, or audio recordings unless specifically needed and you choose to share them.

playcall.support@gmail.com

PlayCall’s in-app Report an issue action offers a template through the iOS share sheet. Audio and route logs are not attached automatically.
