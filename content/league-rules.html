League rules & equipment

Compatibility is not approval.

Confirm the rules for your competition and the equipment you plan to use before stepping onto the field.

01 · Communication

One direction. Applicable rules.

PlayCall provides one-way coach-to-catcher audio. Governing bodies may restrict electronic communication. League, age-division, state-association and tournament rules can vary and change.

Confirm the rules for the season and event with your league or tournament officials. Check where the coach may use the device and which players may receive communication.

02 · Equipment

Verify the headset and microphone.

A headset that works with Bluetooth is not automatically eligible for competition. AirPods, Powerbeats Pro, and other working headphones are not approved for competition merely because they connect.

Confirm the actual devices, how they are worn or used, and any applicable equipment restrictions with the appropriate officials.
